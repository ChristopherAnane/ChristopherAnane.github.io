— The story so far

Early belief.
Years of craft.
Right-time momentum.

I've been creating music since my teens. Around 19, Chicago creative Don C offered to manage me. Years later, Sony Music reached out about my work—two moments that confirmed the music could travel beyond the room where it began.

Today, I bring a deeper creative voice, three released instrumental albums, and a clear faith-centered vision: music that can reach both Christian and mainstream artists without losing its purpose. My production moves through polished pop, R&B, gospel, melodic hip-hop, joyful crossover, rock, and country—with contemporary Christian reference points including Tauren Wells, Forrest Frank, Hulvey, Limoblaze, DOE, and Hollyn, particularly the upbeat pop, R&B, and hip-hop crossover feel of “Nothin' On You.”

ALL ARTISTS WELCOME

No upfront beat fee

My beats are open to Christian and mainstream artists alike, without an upfront fee, when the completed song is about—or inspired by—God and Jesus Christ. My purpose is to help introduce people to the God who already loves them and calls them His own. The artist and I freely collaborate and share mutually agreed producer credit, royalties, publishing, and other backend income.
